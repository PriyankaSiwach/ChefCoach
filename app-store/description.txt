ChefCoach — Your Personal Kitchen Coach

Stop staring at a full fridge wondering what to cook. ChefCoach turns whatever's in your kitchen into delicious, diet-friendly meals in seconds — no wasted food, no meal-planning stress.

─────────────────────────
📸 SCAN YOUR FRIDGE, GET INSTANT RECIPES
─────────────────────────
Point your camera at your fridge or pantry. ChefCoach identifies your ingredients and instantly suggests recipes you can make right now. No grocery run required.

─────────────────────────
🥗 BUILT AROUND YOUR DIET
─────────────────────────
Tell ChefCoach your dietary style once — Vegetarian, Vegan, Keto, Gluten-free, Pescatarian, or Halal — and every suggestion respects it automatically. Add allergies (nuts, dairy, gluten, fish, pork, shellfish) and disliked foods, and they're filtered out everywhere in the app.

─────────────────────────
📊 CALORIE & MACRO TRACKING
─────────────────────────
Log meals with a tap and watch your daily calories and protein stack up toward your personal goal. ChefCoach calculates targets from your profile (age, weight, height, activity level, goal) and shows progress in a clean daily dashboard. Build streaks, earn achievements, and keep momentum week after week.

─────────────────────────
📅 WEEKLY MEAL PLANNING
─────────────────────────
Plan breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the full week in one place. ChefCoach auto-fills your plan with meals that match your diet and calorie target, then generates a smart grocery list — so you always know exactly what to buy.

─────────────────────────
💬 PERSONAL DIET COACH
─────────────────────────
Ask your built-in coach anything: "What should I have for dinner?", "How much protein do I still need today?", or "Give me a high-protein snack under 200 calories." It answers from your real meal library and logged data — no generic advice.

─────────────────────────
❤️ SAVE & ORGANIZE RECIPES
─────────────────────────
Heart any recipe to save it instantly. Saved recipes are always available offline, filtered by diet, and ready to cook from — even without a fridge scan.
